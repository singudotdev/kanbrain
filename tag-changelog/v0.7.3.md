`kb context` says what each accepted decision decided and lists the notes and decisions linked to each task in
progress; the section `kb init` writes gives notes a bullet of their own. No data format change and no API change
(`cargo semver-checks` against v0.7.2: none required): nothing to migrate. `kb init --all`, which `kb update` runs,
brings existing projects' `CLAUDE.md` / `AGENTS.md` up to the new section.

- `kb context` (`core/src/api/context.rs`):
  - Under an accepted decision, `  decided: …`: the first line of its latest acceptance (`Accepted: REASON`, as
    `resolve` writes it), cut at 120 characters. An acceptance with no reason adds no line.
  - Under each task in doing, `  related: …`: the notes and accepted decisions of this repository linked to it,
    either side (`Brain::related`), notes first, three at most, then `· +N more`.
- `kb init` (`core/src/cli/init.rs`): notes get their own bullet (what is worth one, written before finishing a task),
  instead of a sentence inside the decisions bullet.
