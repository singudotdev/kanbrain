kanbrain-core keeps its times with jiff rather than chrono, unmaintained
([RUSTSEC-2026-0543](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2026-0543)); the kanban app's details are resized by
dragging their edge; Rust 1.100 to build. Breaking for `kanbrain-core`'s Rust API only: files are written and read as
before, so nothing to migrate. Decision 0014 in `docs/decisions/`.

- Core (`core/`), breaking:
  - Times are jiff 1.0's: `jiff::Timestamp` in `chrono::DateTime<Local>`'s place (`Task`'s, `Comment`'s, `Session`'s,
    `Activity`'s and `StatusChange`'s times, `model::now`, `model::parse_ts`), `jiff::civil::{DateTime, Date}` in the
    naive types' (`Period::start`, `Period::containing`, `Day::date`), `jiff::SignedDuration` in `chrono::Duration`'s
    (`Report`'s totals, `Day::tracked`, `Event::worked`, `fmt_dur`, `fmt_age`, `fmt_clock`).
  - `model::local(Timestamp) -> ZonedDateTime` and `model::local_moment(civil::DateTime) -> Option<Timestamp>`: a
    civil time turns into an instant by RFC 5545's rule (`AmbiguousTimestamp::compatible`). In the hour a DST change
    repeats, the first of the two on every platform (chrono's `earliest` gave the second on Linux and macOS); in the
    hour it skips, as many minutes past the change (chrono gave none, so a file holding one didn't read).
  - Stored timestamps: still `%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%S%:z` in local time (`TIMESTAMP_FORMAT`); every form read before reads.
  - `model::time::rfc3339`: only what chrono 0.4.45's `parse_from_rfc3339` took reads as a stored time (seconds
    required, `Z`/`z` or `±hh:mm` up to 23:59, `−` U+2212 as minus); other ISO 8601 forms jiff reads (`T10:12Z`,
    `+0200`, `[Europe/Madrid]`) stay text, as before. A sign apart from a hand-typed year (`+ 2026-…`) reads no time.
  - `fmt_ts`: an offset with seconds (local mean time, before 1901 in most zones) is written to the nearest minute, at
    the same moment, so every version reads it back.
  - `TimeZone::system()`: a `TZ` naming no zone gives UTC (chrono fell back to the system's setting); on Windows, `TZ`
    is taken, with jiff's bundled time zone database.
- Kanban app (`gui/`):
  - `ui::drawer::framed`: the details beside the board or list take their width from a drag of their left edge
    (`BrainView::drawer_width`, `resizing`), from `NARROWEST` (320 px) to what leaves the board `BOARD_LEAST` (360
    px) (`drawer::width`); a double-click puts them back to `WIDTH` (420 px). Kept while the app runs.
  - `ui::calendar::event_time`: a session's span in the day view, its start in a week's or month's column.
- Build: `rust-version = "1.100"` (was 1.88); CI on `dtolnay/rust-toolchain@1.100`.
- Dependencies: jiff 1.0.1 in chrono's place (GPUI still pulls chrono in; its advisory is ignored in `deny.toml` for
  it alone); rustix 1.1.6 (RUSTSEC-2026-0486); pulldown-cmark 0.13.6 (RUSTSEC-2026-0433).
