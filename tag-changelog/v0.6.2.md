The kanban app's editor merges what `kb` changed while it was open, line by line, and asks only about the lines both
changed: yours, theirs or both for each. The form shows a title `kb` gave meanwhile, to take. App only: `kanbrain-core`
and `kb` don't change, nor does the data, so nothing needs migrating.

- Kanban app (`gui/`):
  - `text::merge` (new): `merge(base, mine, theirs) -> Merged::{Clean(String), Conflicted(Merge)}`, a three-way line
    merge on `similar` 3.2's `TextMerge`; each text gets a `\n` after its last line, so it merges like any other.
    `untangled` applies both sides of a conflict whose changes only touch (no line of `base` changed on both); lines
    added at one place on both sides, or within lines the other changed, stay `Region::Conflict { mine, theirs, pick }`.
    `Merge::{conflicts, text, pick, pick_at, pick_rest, go}`; `Pick::{Mine, Theirs, Both}`.
  - `Board::save_edit` over a text `kb` changed since `Edit::base` (`merge_meanwhile`): a clean merge saves
    (`description saved, merged with the change made meanwhile`), or says `nothing to save: … has your changes
    already`; lines both changed set `Edit::merge` and stop it (`… changed meanwhile: pick yours or theirs where you
    both changed it`). It stopped at any change, for saving again to replace it all.
  - The conflicts in the editor's place (`ui::dialogs::merge`): yours beside theirs, the lines settled between them,
    who changed a description and when (from its activity), `(removed)` or `(an empty line)` for a side that shows
    nothing. Keys (`Board::key_merge`): `m`/`t`/`b` in either case, `tab`/`↓` and `shift+tab`/`↑` between conflicts,
    `enter` **Use the result** (`use_merge`: the merged text in the editor, `Edit::base` the text it was picked over),
    `esc` **Yours for the rest** (`keep_mine`), `ctrl+d` **Discard changes**; typing, paste and selection are off.
  - `Board::merge_again` on a reload: the conflicts of a text `kb` changed again are merged again (`… changed again:
    pick again …`, or `it all merges now, esc saves it`, or `it's theirs as it is now, esc closes`); a comment `kb`
    moved (`same_comment`) drops them and gives the text typed back, for the save to stop over.
  - The form (`Form::theirs_title`): a title `kb` changed while the form changed it too stops the save (`#N's title
    changed meanwhile, to “X”: save again to keep yours, ctrl+t to take it`) and shows under the field, refreshed on a
    reload; `ctrl+t` or **Use it** (`take_theirs_title`) takes it.
  - `Edit::note` and `Form::note`: a save that stops after **Tell the agent?** was answered asks it again with the
    note typed (`Pending::note`).
- Dependencies: `similar` 3.2 (`gui` only; features `std` and `text`; Apache-2.0).
